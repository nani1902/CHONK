# Phase 0 benchmark protocol (pre-registered)

This file was committed **before** any benchmark result existed. Metrics and
thresholds must not change after results are seen. If they must, the change
goes in `REPORT.md` with the reason, and the verdict under the original rules
is still reported.

The CHONK engine is frozen for the benchmark at the commit that adds this file.

## Question

On the small targets real portals use, does CHONK produce files that are
**usable** (under the limit *and* still legible) meaningfully more often than
the strategies common compressors use? If not, a general-public web app built
on "fits, and verified legible" has no wedge.

## Methods compared

The competitors are **proxies for their strategies**, not the real websites.
Hosted tools can't be scripted from here, and uploading documents to them would
defeat the point. Real sites may do better or worse than these proxies.

| ID | Method | Stands in for |
|---|---|---|
| `gs_ebook` | Ghostscript `-dPDFSETTINGS=/ebook`, one pass | The default "compress PDF" preset in many tools (150 dpi JPEG) |
| `gs_screen` | Ghostscript `/screen`, one pass | The "strong compression" preset (72 dpi) |
| `gs_ladder` | First of `/printer`, `/ebook`, `/screen`, `/screen` at 50 dpi, `/screen` at 36 dpi that fits the target | Tools that retry harder presets until the file is small enough |
| `raster_target` | Rasterise every page; for 150, 120, 100, 72, 50 dpi in turn, binary-search the highest JPEG quality (10–95) that fits; keep the first resolution with a fit | The common in-browser "compress PDF to X KB" design (pdf.js + canvas + pdf-lib). Removes the text layer |
| `chonk` | `chonk.engine.compress_bytes` with default options (colour kept, min 72 dpi) | CHONK |

## Corpus

Synthetic documents generated by `bench/corpus.py` with fixed seeds. No real
documents, faces or identities are used.

| ID | Document | Why |
|---|---|---|
| `passport_scan` | 2 pages, colour, 300 dpi flatbed scan of a passport-style data page on A4: guilloche background, a synthetic portrait block, 7–9 pt fields, two machine-readable-zone lines | The privacy use case; small text on a busy background |
| `marksheet_scan` | 3 pages, grayscale, 300 dpi: table of 8 pt text, a stamp and a signature | Exam portals, 100–300 KB limits |
| `phone_photo` | 1 page: a 12 MP phone photo of a printed page (skew, uneven light, sensor noise) | How most people actually "scan" |
| `digital_with_photo` | 2 pages, born-digital: vector text plus one large embedded photo | Rasterising tools destroy vector text; CHONK should keep it |
| `certificate_scan` | 1 page, colour, 300 dpi: border, coloured seal, signature, 10–12 pt text | Certificates for scholarship and exam portals |

## Targets

100 KB, 200 KB, 300 KB, 1 MB and 2 MB (decimal), from `portal_requirements.md`.
A (document, target) cell is **excluded** when the source already fits the target.

## Metrics, per (document, target, method)

- **fits**: output bytes ≤ target. A method that errors or produces no output
  counts as not fitting.
- **ocr_loss_pct** (primary quality metric): OCR both files with Tesseract at
  300 dpi on every page. Take words Tesseract reads on the original with
  confidence ≥ 90. `ocr_loss_pct` = 100 × (characters in those words that don't
  reappear, identically and in order, on the output) ÷ (characters in those
  words). This is CHONK's own `ocr.compare_words` logic. It is an independent
  check, because CHONK's search optimises SSIM, not OCR.
- **worst_ssim**: worst-page SSIM at 150 dpi. Secondary only: CHONK optimises
  SSIM, so it is biased in CHONK's favour.
- **text_layer_kept**: for born-digital input, does the output still hold
  extractable text (pdfium character count > 0)?
- **seconds**: wall-clock time.

A document whose original yields fewer than 200 confident OCR characters is
excluded from OCR metrics and reported as such.

## Primary endpoint

**Usable fit** = fits **and** `ocr_loss_pct` ≤ 2.0.

**Usable-fit rate** = usable fits ÷ included cells, per method.
**Best competitor** = the non-CHONK method with the highest usable-fit rate.

## Decision rule

- **GO: the wedge is real.** Either
  - CHONK's usable-fit rate ≥ the best competitor's + 15 percentage points, or
  - in cells where both CHONK and the best competitor fit, CHONK's
    `ocr_loss_pct` is lower in at least half of them, and the median improvement
    is ≥ 2 points.
- **NO-GO: no wedge.** The best competitor's usable-fit rate is within 5 points
  of CHONK's, and the median `ocr_loss_pct` difference in shared-fit cells is
  under 1 point.
- **WEAK** otherwise: a difference exists but is small. Proceed only with the
  agent-native niche and portal presets, not a general "better compressor" pitch.

## Checks against fooling ourselves

- Visually inspect crops of the machine-readable zone and the smallest text for
  every method at the hardest target where it fits. The report includes them.
- If Tesseract reads fewer than 200 confident characters on any original, the
  corpus is too hard to OCR and must be fixed **before** any method is compared.
- Report every cell, not just aggregates.
